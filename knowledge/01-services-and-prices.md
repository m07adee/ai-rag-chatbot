# Services and Prices

Lumina Dental Studio is a fictional demo clinic used to showcase this chatbot. All prices are in US dollars and include VAT. Final prices are confirmed after a free examination.

## General dentistry
- Check-up and consultation with digital X-ray: free for new patients, $30 for returning patients.
- Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing): $60, takes about 45 minutes.
- Tooth-colored composite filling: from $70 per tooth.
- Root canal treatment: from $250 (front teeth) to $400 (molars), usually 1-2 visits.
- Tooth extraction: simple $50, surgical / wisdom tooth from $150.

## Cosmetic dentistry
- In-office teeth whitening (Philips Zoom): $250, one 90-minute session, results up to 8 shades lighter.
- Home whitening kit with custom trays: $180.
- Porcelain veneers (E-max): $280 per tooth. A full smile design (16-20 veneers) is planned in a digital simulation before any work starts.
- Zirconia crown: $220 per tooth.

## Implants and orthodontics
- Dental implant (Straumann, Swiss) including abutment: $650 per implant; the crown on top is $220.
- All-on-4 full arch: from $4,500 per jaw.
- Clear aligners (Invisalign): from $2,800 for a full treatment; a free 3D scan shows the expected result.
- Metal braces: $1,500; ceramic braces: $1,900.

## Children's dentistry
- Children's check-up: $20. Fissure sealant: $25 per tooth. Fluoride application: $20.
- Our pediatric dentist sees children from age 3.

## Payment
We accept cash, all major credit cards and bank transfer. Treatments over $1,000 can be paid in up to 6 interest-free monthly installments.
