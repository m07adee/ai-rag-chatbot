# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I book an appointment?
You can book by phone or WhatsApp, or ask this chat to connect you with our team and leave your name and number. We usually confirm appointments within 2 working hours.

## Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. The first examination, digital X-ray and treatment plan are free for new patients. There is no obligation to start treatment.

## Does teeth whitening hurt?
Most patients feel no pain. Some feel temporary sensitivity for 24-48 hours, and we apply a desensitizing gel after the session.

## How long do veneers last?
Porcelain (E-max) veneers usually last 10-15 years with good care. We give a 5-year warranty on veneers and crowns.

## How long does an implant take?
The implant is placed in one visit of about 1 hour. The bone needs 2-3 months to heal before the final crown is fitted. A temporary tooth can be placed in the meantime.

## Do you treat children?
Yes, our pediatric dentist treats children from age 3 in a child-friendly room.

## Do you offer sedation?
Yes, conscious sedation is available for anxious patients and longer surgeries for an extra $150.

## What should I do in a dental emergency?
During opening hours, call the clinic and we will see you the same day. On Sundays and holidays, call the emergency line. If there is heavy bleeding or swelling that affects breathing, go to the nearest hospital emergency room.

## Can I get a treatment plan before travelling?
Yes. Send recent X-rays or clear photos of your teeth on WhatsApp and our dentist will prepare a preliminary plan and estimate for free.
