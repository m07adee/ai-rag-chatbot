# Policies, Warranty and Insurance

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations of surgical appointments may be charged a $30 fee.

## Warranty
- Implants: lifetime warranty on the Straumann implant body, 5 years on the crown.
- Veneers and crowns: 5 years.
- Fillings: 2 years.
Warranties require a check-up at least once a year.

## Insurance
We work with private health insurers that cover dental care, including Allianz, AXA and Acıbadem Sigorta. We issue detailed invoices that international patients can submit to their own insurance for reimbursement. We do not work with the public SGK system.

## Hygiene and safety
All instruments are sterilized in class-B autoclaves and single-use materials are used whenever possible.

## Privacy
Patient records and X-rays are kept confidential and are only shared with the patient's written consent.
