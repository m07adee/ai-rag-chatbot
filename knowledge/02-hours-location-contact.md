# Opening Hours, Location and Contact

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 09:00 – 20:00
- Saturday: 10:00 – 16:00
- Sunday: closed
- Public holidays: closed. For dental emergencies on Sundays and holidays, call the emergency line.

## Address
Lumina Dental Studio (fictional demo clinic), Bağdat Avenue No. 120, Kadıköy, Istanbul, Turkey.
The clinic is a 5-minute walk from the Göztepe Marmaray station. Free parking is available for patients in the building garage.

## Languages
Our team speaks English, Turkish and Arabic, so international patients can be treated in their own language.

## International patients
We help international patients plan their visit: airport pick-up from Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen is free for treatments over $2,000, and we can recommend partner hotels near the clinic. Most implant and veneer treatments need 2 trips or one stay of 5-7 days.
